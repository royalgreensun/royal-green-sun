About Royal Green Sun

Engineered with purpose.

Royal Green Sun Renewable Energy is an engineering and technology company focused on creating practical solutions through design, analysis, innovation and technology.

Who we are

Engineering across disciplines.

Royal Green Sun brings together engineering design, computational analysis and technology to work on diverse technical challenges.

Our work spans mechanical design, CAD, CFD, FEA, thermal analysis, aerospace and UAV applications, product development and renewable energy.

We approach engineering challenges through a combination of technical understanding, digital modelling, simulation and practical problem-solving.
